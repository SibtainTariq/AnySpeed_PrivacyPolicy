AnySpeed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AnySpeed ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our GPS speedometer application. AnySpeed works on your device, with no account, login, or server of our own.

A. Information We Do Not Collect

AnySpeed does not require registration or an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information. We do not operate any backend server.

B. Location Data (GPS)

AnySpeed's core function — measuring speed, distance, and pace — requires access to your device's GPS location ("Fine Location"). This data:

  • Is processed entirely on your device, in real time.
  • Is not sent by the App to us or stored on any server.
  • Is only used while the App is open and active. Location access is requested through the standard Android permission prompt, and you may revoke it at any time in your device Settings, though this will disable speed tracking.

Separately, Google AdMob may receive your IP address, from which approximate location can be inferred, as described in Section C.

C. Advertising

AnySpeed is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), IP address, general device information, and app usage and ad interaction data. This collection is performed by Google directly, not by us.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required by law (such as GDPR in the EU/EEA or UK), you will be shown a consent prompt so you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the App's Support screen ("Ad Privacy Options"). On Android you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device Settings under Google → Ads.

D. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is the advertising-related data described above, which is collected by Google AdMob and its ad partners to show and measure ads.

E. Data Retention

Since AnySpeed does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no personal data for us to retain. Your trip stats (speed, distance) exist only in the App's memory while it is open and are cleared when you close the App or tap Reset Trip. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies.

F. Children's Privacy

AnySpeed is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App and provided information to us, please contact us using the details below.

G. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of data associated with you. Because AnySpeed does not collect or store personal data outside of your own device, most such requests can be fulfilled by uninstalling the App or resetting your Advertising ID in your device settings. For ad-related data held by Google, you can manage it here: https://myadcenter.google.com

For any other privacy request, contact us using the details below.

H. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. Changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
